How the categories work (for instructors)

What it describes uses roots, suffixes, full terms, phrases and pictures. Suffixes often tell you the category: -ectomy names a surgery, -itis a condition, -scopy an exam, -algia a symptom. Prefixes mostly describe amount, speed or position, so they appear only in Kind of word rounds.

Some items fit more than one bin (tachycardia is a sign, and can also be a diagnosis). Each item has one main bin plus any bins that are also accepted. An accepted bin counts as correct, and the feedback explains the difference.

In Learn, a card in the wrong bin bounces back with a clue, and the answer is shown after two misses. In Timed test, every drop is final: a wrong card is marked red and moved to the right bin, so the score is true first-try accuracy.

All items are drafts. They need instructor approval before students use them.
