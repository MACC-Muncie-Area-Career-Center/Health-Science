Start in Learn mode

No clock. Wrong answers come with a clue, and every right answer shows the meaning and how to say the word.

Then test yourself

Timed modes check what you remember. Your results show which categories you know and which words to review.

Works on any device

Play on a Chromebook, laptop, tablet or phone. Drag with a mouse or finger, or tap a card and then tap where it goes.
