How Decode the Unknown works (for instructors)

The skill. Students meet a term they may not have studied and work out its meaning from its parts, the way they will on the job. Every term is from Acquiring Medical Language; meanings, splits and pronunciations are written by MedDecode.

Clues cost points. A word is worth 5 points with no clues. Clue 1 splits the word into its parts. The next clues give each part's meaning, starting from the end of the word (the suffix usually says what kind of word it is), then the prefix, then the roots. A correct answer earns 5 minus the clues used (at least 1).

The wrong choices share parts with the answer (for gastritis: stomach pain, inflammation of the colon), so students have to decode every part, not just spot one.

Covered so far. Set this to the chapters your class has finished, then press Copy student link. Words then come from chapters the class hasn't reached yet, and any part already covered is marked You know this. On the MedDecode site, words a student hasn't met in Daily 10 are chosen first.

Guess first hides the four choices until the student (or the class) has said a guess out loud. It turns on automatically in Classroom mode. With Team mode on, each word is one team's turn, worth the same points as solo play.
