How cuts are judged (for instructors)

A cut must fall exactly on the border between two word parts. A cut inside a part is marked wrong, and so is a dissection that leaves two parts joined.

Combining vowels: both common textbook styles are accepted. For cardiology, students may cut cardi | o | logy, cardio | logy or cardi | ology. Between two roots the vowel stays with the first root: gastro | enter | itis is accepted, gastr | oenter | itis is not. Some words list extra accepted splits, such as tachy | cardia alongside tachy | card | ia.

Guidance gets more specific with each try: first the type of part the student cut into, then the part itself. After two misses a Show me button appears.

Scoring: a correct first try earns 100 points plus a 50-point first-cut bonus, and every third clean dissection in a row adds 25 more. A second try earns 60, later tries 30, and Show me earns 0.
