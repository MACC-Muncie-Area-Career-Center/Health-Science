About this list (for instructors)

This page is built from the MedDecode master list, the same list that feeds Tiles, Dissect, Sort and Build. When terms are added or corrected, this page updates with the games.

Chapters follow Acquiring Medical Language (Jones and Cavanagh, McGraw Hill Education, 3rd edition). Entries marked from the book word lists are words from the book's chapter quick-reference tables (Chapters 1–13); their definitions, word parts and pronunciations were written for MedDecode, not copied from the book. They are drafts: instructors flag anything that needs fixing.

Programs and body regions are first drafts. All entries are drafts until instructors approve them in the content spreadsheet.
