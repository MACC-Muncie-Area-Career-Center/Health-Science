How Translator works (for instructors)

Chart → Patient: students read a short chart note and choose the plain-language meaning of each marked term, then see the whole note rewritten the way they would explain it to a patient (with a Read aloud button). Patient → Chart: students read the plain version and choose or type the medical term for each marked phrase, then see the finished chart note. "Type" applies to Patient → Chart and accepts a one-letter slip on longer words, with a spelling note.

The notes are built from the MedDecode master list: each one combines two or three textbook terms from the same body system into a chief complaint, exam finding, diagnosis, test, plan or referral. Patient ages and sexes are made up and match the terms. These are practice notes, not real records or clinical advice. Use the Topic bar to focus on a chapter, body region or program.

Talk it through: after each note, have partners read the patient version aloud as if explaining it at the bedside, a good speaking check for CNA, EMT and CCMA students.

Team mode: each marked term is one team's turn.
