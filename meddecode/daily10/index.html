How Daily 10 works (for instructors)

Spaced review. Each word sits in one of five boxes. A correct answer moves it up a box and pushes its next review further out (1, 2, 4, 7, then 14 days). A miss sends it back to box 1, so it returns tomorrow. Each day's 10 starts with the words that are due, then adds new words from the chosen topic, dealt evenly across chapters.

Question types get harder as a word moves up: new words ask for the meaning or a word part; middle boxes ask students to pick the term from its definition or from hearing it; top boxes ask them to spell it. Missed words come back once at the end of the set as a quick fix (that second try doesn't change the box).

Topics. Pick a chapter, body region or program and press Copy student link to post a focused link in Schoology. New words then come from that topic; words that are due for review still appear from any topic.

Privacy. Progress is stored only in the student's browser on that device. Nothing is sent anywhere. For credit, students can screenshot the "Daily 10 complete" card with the date and score. Students who switch devices can use the progress code to carry their boxes over.

Class warm-up is for the classroom screen: 10 questions from the chosen topic, no saved progress. With Team mode on, each answer is one team's play.
